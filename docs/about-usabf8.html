About Us

AST Research Group

Our research group merges a multidisciplinar team which tackles key investigation lines of telematic services. We mainly work in distributed systems where we have experience in scalable systems (cloud computing, peer-to-peer), web-based infrastructures and mobile and wireless communications. In this field the group is very active and has participated in european and national research projects.
